How each fixing is made

  • Every model scoring 25 or more on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5 qualifies: 25–44 for SITB, 45 and above for SITF.
  • Each model's price is its list price blended 3 input tokens to 1 output, rescaled to the tier anchor (30 or 55) in proportion to its score.
  • Models are weighted by OpenRouter request share, no lab above 30% of a tier. SITC is 70% SITB and 30% SITF by token count.
  • "Cheapest" is the lowest-cost qualifying model: what a redeemable SIT unit would be paid out in.

Scenarios and sources

  • Scenario paths use Xing's mean-reverting jump-diffusion model, 2,000 simulations from the latest fixing. They are illustrations, not forecasts.
  • No provider publishes a capability-adjusted price history, so this record starts at the first fixing and grows by one point a day. Prices come from OpenRouter, which publishes current prices only.
  • Raw data: /api/fixings (all fixings) and /api/fixings?date=YYYY-MM-DD (one fixing with every constituent).
  • Sources: Xing (2026), AA Intelligence Index, OpenRouter prices, OpenRouter rankings.
